Make it again

Recipe by the kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1¾ cups confectioners’ sugar
  • 1¼ cups almond flour
  • ⅔ cup egg whites
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1–3 drops food colouring (optional)

Directions

  1. Sift the sugar and almond flour together, twice.
  2. Whip the whites to stiff, glossy peaks; fold in the dry ingredients.
  3. Pipe rounds, rest 30 minutes, then bake low and slow.
  4. Fill, sandwich, and chill overnight before serving.
